# BitCapital Public Overview

BitCapital is the applied implementation and research layer of the broader WLRT / WaveCounter ecosystem.

It exists as an internal environment for testing, documenting, and developing structural liquidity methods based on WLRT and WaveCounter™ Liquidity Pool Management System (LPM).

At the current stage, BitCapital operates the BitCapital Research Pool as an internal liquidity environment using its own funds. This environment is used to study role-based asset interaction, reserve logic, structural fragility, regime changes, and the practical behavior of liquidity under live market conditions.

BitCapital is not presented here as a public investment fund, client pool, or financial advisory service. Its role in this repository is methodological, architectural, and research-oriented.

Within the broader ecosystem:
- WLRT provides the theoretical framework;
- WaveCounter provides the applied system logic;
- WaveCounter IP S.L. is intended to hold and develop the relevant intellectual property;
- BitCapital provides applied implementation and internal research practice;
- KEFEN functions as a structural and experimental token component within the evolving architecture.

The purpose of BitCapital’s public documentation is to describe this internal research environment clearly, document the conceptual role of KEFEN, and provide a structured basis for future operator-oriented, educational, and licensing layers.

All materials in this repository are published for research, educational, methodological, and technical documentation purposes only.

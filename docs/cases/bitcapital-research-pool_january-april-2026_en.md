# BitCapital Research Pool: consolidated report for January–April 2026

## Period overview

This document presents a consolidated report on the management of the BitCapital Research Pool for the period from December 2025 to April 2026 (12.12.2025 – 30.04.2026). It combines the results of four consecutive stages: initialization and model verification (January), maximization of effectiveness in a consolidating environment (February), an increase in market exposure (March), and subsequent structural stabilization with risk control (April).

Throughout the observed interval, the market environment was characterized as consolidating: the prior downward trend was considered broken, while a sustained upward trend had not yet formed. This defined the management logic: no reliance on selling into a clear trend, an emphasis on structural rebalancing rather than directional bets, control of total pool loading, and the use of USDT as a liquidity buffer.

## Market environment

During January–April 2026, the environment retained the following structure:

- the previous downward trend was treated as no longer continuing in its former shape;
- a stable upward trend had not yet been confirmed;
- the dominant regime was consolidation.

Under these conditions, pool management focused not on predicting market direction, but on selecting structure: which asset weights and which volume of the liquidity buffer were acceptable for operating inside ranges.

## Management parameters

### January: initial configuration

At the initialization stage, an intentionally limited structure was used:

- BTC – up to 18%;
- SOL – up to 36%;
- KEFEN – up to 36%;
- total loading – no more than 72% of the pool;
- liquidity reserve – at least 28%.

This configuration reflected the task of testing the model in a consolidating environment without moving to maximum risk exposure.

### February: extended loading

After confirming the base model in January, the parameters were expanded:

- BTC – up to 25%;
- SOL – up to 36%;
- KEFEN – up to 20%;
- total loading – up to 80%.

In other words, the system was moved into a more intensive mode of using the consolidating environment, while still preserving limits both per asset and for total loading.

### March–April: flexible rebalancing and the role of USDT

In March and April, management took a more flexible form, described by:

- redistribution of asset weights;
- varying the position size in BTC and SOL;
- strengthening the role of USDT as a stabilizing liquidity buffer;
- a gradual shift from increasing exposure (March) to reducing exposure and fixing part of the result (April).

Thus, the model moved from a cautious initial configuration through a higher-loading phase to a structural stabilization phase.

## Strategy evolution

The evolution of the strategy over the period can be summarized as follows:

- **January:** limited loading, model testing on a small base, formation of an initial positive result and verification of structural manageability.
- **February:** more aggressive realization within consolidation, high return under extended loading limits, confirmation of effectiveness on a larger base.
- **March:** increase in market exposure, growth through larger positions in risk assets while preserving structural control.
- **April:** risk reduction, increase in the USDT share, partial fixing of results in BTC and SOL, continued central role of KEFEN.

In other words, the strategy was not static: as observations and capital accumulated, the system moved from a conservative regime to a more active one and then to structural stabilization.

## Key management principles

Across the entire period, several principles consistently shaped pool management:

- no reliance on selling as a primary tactic in an uncertain trend environment;
- a focus on structural rebalancing rather than on predicting market direction;
- control of total pool loading and constraints on concentration in individual assets via limits;
- use of USDT as a stabilizing liquidity buffer;
- adaptation of asset weights to the current environment and transitions between "increase exposure" and "reduce risk" regimes.

These principles define the frame within which each individual monthly result is interpreted.

## Overall conclusion

Over the January–April 2026 interval, the BitCapital Research Pool strategy demonstrated sustained profitability and the ability to adapt to different phases of a single consolidating environment. Capital growth was accompanied by a controlled level of risk and deliberate structural changes in the pool — from cautious initial loading to a phase of more active market participation and subsequent stabilization through a higher liquidity share.

The key outcome of the period lies not only in cumulative value growth, but also in confirming that the pool management model can operate without relying on explicit forecasts of market direction, instead using structural adaptation and active management of system fragility.

# BitCapital Research Pool  
## First Peripheral Pool Management Report  
### Period: June 2026

---

## 1. Period overview

The first peripheral pool closed the reporting month with a positive financial result. The pool was launched through a direct inflow of 20,000 USDT, so the starting structure of the period was fully liquid and did not contain a mixed asset composition.

By the end of the month, the total pool value reached 20,912 USDT, corresponding to a profit of 912 USDT and a return of 4.56% for the period.

## 4. Result interpretation

The result of the first peripheral pool shows that the BitCapital model can be reproduced not only in the core pool, but also in an additional management layer. The positive return was achieved while preserving the role-based asset structure and without turning the peripheral pool into an aggressive one-directional market bet.

It is especially important that KEFEN became the largest component of the structure by the end of the period. This is consistent with the BitCapital logic in which KEFEN performs the functions of an adaptive layer, a store of purchasing power, and a liquidity redistribution component within the system.

The presence of 4,000 USDT supports a liquidity buffer and preserves flexibility for further management. BTC and SOL continue to perform their roles of stability and development without dominating the overall architecture.

---

## 5. Conclusions

The first peripheral pool closed the month with a positive result of 4.56%, confirming the operability of the model at a smaller scale.

The end-of-period structure shows:

- preservation of a liquidity layer through USDT;  
- a significant role of KEFEN as a strategic adaptive component;  
- the presence of BTC as the stability layer;  
- the presence of SOL as the development layer.

For BitCapital, this matters not only as a monthly result, but also as confirmation that the principles of liquidity management and role-based asset allocation can be transferred from the Core Pool to a Peripheral Pool without losing the internal logic of the system.

---

## 6. Summary

June 2026 can be regarded as a successful initial period for the first peripheral pool. The pool started from a fully liquid base of 20,000 USDT and closed the month at 20,912 USDT, producing a positive result while preserving the architectural principles of BitCapital.

This makes the first peripheral pool an important practical confirmation of the scalability of the BitCapital model within the broader liquidity management system.

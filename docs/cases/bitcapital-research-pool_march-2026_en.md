# BitCapital Research Pool: structural note for March 2026

## Period overview

This document presents a revised version of the report on BitCapital Research Pool management for March 2026. The material is framed as a research-oriented note in which the result of the period is considered through changes in pool structure, redistribution of liquidity, the character of market exposure, and the limits of interpretation.

March 2026 may be described as a phase of more active pool loading compared with the preceding periods. The management logic shifted toward increasing the share of risk assets while preserving the overall manageability of the structure.

## Structural dynamics

The key changes during the period may be described as follows:

- **USDT:** the reserve declined from 14,021 USDT to 12,352 USDT, indicating partial use of the liquidity buffer.
- **KEFEN:** the value of the position increased due to the change in market price and provided the main contribution to the result for the period.
- **BTC:** the position was increased from 0.164 to 0.177 BTC, and the simultaneous rise in price amplified the total exposure.
- **SOL:** the position was increased from 88.7 to 99.23 SOL under a more moderate price dynamic.

Taken together, these changes make it possible to interpret March as a period in which the pool structure was moved into a more active mode of market participation. In other words, the positive result was associated not only with favorable revaluation of existing assets, but also with a more pronounced loading of the pool.

## Management observation

March unfolded in a mode of active pool loading: positions in BTC and SOL were increased, and part of the liquidity reserve was used. The main contribution to return was provided by KEFEN and BTC, which reinforced the role of risk assets in the overall structure of the result.

From a management perspective, this means that the strategy for the period was oriented toward growth through expanded market exposure together with a moderate reduction in the share of liquidity. Accordingly, the positive result of March should be understood as the outcome of a controlled increase in risk rather than as the consequence of passively maintaining the previous structure.

## Interpretation of the result

The March result shows that the observed management model is capable of moving from a more reserved and moderate configuration to a more actively loaded one without losing internal manageability. However, such adaptation is accompanied by a change in the fragility profile: a lower liquidity share and a higher share of risk assets increase the sensitivity of the pool to changes in the environment.

A more precise interpretation of the result is that sustained capital growth in March was achieved under a controlled increase in risk. This distinction matters, because the result itself does not imply automatic preservation of the same dynamics in the event of a regime shift or deterioration in liquidity conditions.

## Limits of interpretation

This note relates to a specific period and a specific pool structure. Therefore, the recorded result:
- does not constitute a forecast of future returns;
- does not guarantee repetition of similar dynamics under a different structure or in a different environment;
- depends on the combination of market revaluation of assets and a higher pool loading;
- should be interpreted only in the context of the observed period and the specific management configuration.

## 1. Executive Summary

From January through September 2026, BitCapital's managed liquidity pools recorded a combined positive financial result of **80,017.69 USDT**.

During the reporting period, the operating architecture evolved from a single Nuclear Liquidity Pool to independently managed Peripheral Liquidity Pools.

The first annuity period of the new Rita Pool began in September and closed on October 12, generating **2,574.38 USDT**.

The aggregate result of all listed completed periods, including this subsequent annuity period, amounts to **82,592.07 USDT**.

This extended figure is not attributable solely to the nine calendar months ending September 30.

Reported results reflect changes in managed portfolio values, including market revaluation. They are presented before profit distribution and do not represent BitCapital's net corporate earnings.

## 9. Scientific and Analytical Foundations

The theoretical foundation of the system is **Wave Liquidity Redistribution Theory (WLRT)**, developed by Petr Popov.

WLRT studies structural liquidity redistribution processes in adaptive systems.

**WaveCounter** is a separate applied analytical architecture designed to observe structural market states, transitions between states, and the development of directional movements.

BitCapital uses analytical results to support the organization and management of liquidity pools.

Specific asset allocation and rebalancing decisions are made by operators within the established mandates and contractual terms of each pool.

## 10. Operational Architecture

During the reporting period, BitCapital developed a management framework incorporating:

- Individual management periods.
- Period-opening tickets.
- Operational rebalancing tickets.
- Period-closing tickets.
- Financial result recording.
- Profit distribution.
- Assessment of portfolio structure after profit withdrawal.
- Preparation for the next management period.

This structure supports documented decision-making and comparison of results across independent pools.

## 11. Transition to the Annuity Model

BitCapital's organizational architecture evolved in successive stages.

The initial structure was based on the Nuclear Pool.

The next stage introduced independently managed Peripheral Pools.

Implementation of the annuity model began in September 2026.

**October 2026 is a transitional month**, during which elements of the previous and new management systems operate simultaneously.

**Beginning in November 2026, all pools are scheduled to operate under the annuity model.**

The new model introduces regular financial obligations consisting of interest payments and gradual principal repayment.

Future performance will be assessed separately, taking into account payment schedules, changes in outstanding obligations, and the composition of managed assets.

## 12. Methodology and Limitations

All financial results are expressed in USDT.

The base capital of each pool is maintained at its established amount unless a capital change is explicitly confirmed.

Minor exchange-rate valuation differences are not treated as changes in base capital.

Results reflect managed portfolio performance before profit distribution.

Aggregate pool profit is not equivalent to BitCapital's net corporate earnings.

Market revaluation is included in the reported results for the respective periods.

The annuity period completed on October 12 is presented separately from the official nine-month reporting result.

Historical performance does not guarantee future returns.

## 13. Conclusion

For January–September 2026, BitCapital recorded **80,017.69 USDT in combined positive financial results** from managed liquidity pools.

Third-quarter results amounted to **28,714.69 USDT**.

In September, three peripheral pools operating under the previous model generated **15,451.14 USDT** on a combined base capital of 123,792 USDT, corresponding to a weighted return of **12.48%**.

The first annuity period of the new Rita Pool closed on October 12 with a financial result of **2,574.38 USDT**, or **12.87%**.

The aggregate of all listed completed periods, including this subsequent event, is **82,592.07 USDT**.

The principal organizational achievement was the transition from a single Nuclear Pool to multiple independent Peripheral Pools, followed by the introduction of an annuity-based management architecture.

October is designated as the transitional month, with all pools scheduled to move to the new model in November.

# BitCapital Research Pool: structural note for January 2026

## Period overview

This document is a revised version of the brief internal note on BitCapital Research Pool management for January 2026. It is presented in the format of a research-oriented note: the emphasis is shifted from a short operational summary to a description of the environment, management logic, period result, and limits of interpretation.

The observed interval covers the period from 12.12.2025 to 31.01.2026. At this stage, the task was to manage the pool within a consolidating market environment, maintain moderate loading of the structure, and preserve a liquidity reserve for subsequent scaling.

## Market context

During the period, the environment was described as consolidation. The prior downward trend was no longer treated as continuing in its previous form, but a full upward trend had not yet been established.

This meant that pool management was not conducted in a regime of following a clear directional move, but in a regime of operating inside ranges. In such an environment, control of loading, preservation of a liquidity buffer, and limitation of excessive concentration of risk became key elements.

## Pool management parameters

Within the observed environment, the following loading limits for purchases using USDT were applied:

| Asset | Allowable loading |
|---|---:|
| BTC | up to 18% |
| SOL | up to 36% |
| KEFEN | up to 36% |
| **Total loading** | **no more than 72% of the pool** |
| **Liquidity reserve** | **at least 28%** |

This configuration shows that the structure was built not as a fully loaded position, but as a controlled scheme with a deliberately preserved liquidity buffer. This is particularly important in an environment without a confirmed directional move.

Substantively, this means the period was treated not only as a test of return, but also as a stage at which the pool already began to cover part of the operating load while maintaining a positive residual result.

## Management observation

January, including the closing part of December, unfolded in a mode of operating within a consolidating environment, maintaining moderate pool loading, and forming an initial return. The key observation was not so much the absolute value of the result as the confirmation that the management structure being used remained functional without requiring changes to its base model.

In practice, this was expressed in the ability to proceed to the next stage with an increased base of 30,000 USDT without changing the underlying management grid. In other words, the result of the period was interpreted as a basis not for changing the logic, but for scaling the already validated scheme.

## Transition to the next period

Following January, the base for the next period was set at 30,000 USDT. The pool management structure and allocation logic did not require fundamental revision.

From a research perspective, this is important: the move to a larger base was driven not by a change of model, but by recognition of its functional robustness within the observed environment.

## Limits of interpretation

This note describes a specific early stage of pool management in a particular environment. Therefore, the recorded result:

- does not constitute a forecast of future returns;
- does not guarantee repetition of similar dynamics at a larger scale;
- does not eliminate market fragility or the risk of regime change;
- should be interpreted only in the context of a consolidating regime and the specified loading parameters.

## 1. Monthly overview

May 2026 was a month of strong growth for the BitCapital Research Pool, with the pool value increasing while the core liquidity management architecture remained unchanged. Over the period, the pool moved from a notional base of 100,000 to 117,731 USDT equivalent, which corresponds to a 17.73% result at the pool level before subsequent profit withdrawals.

This performance was achieved without changing the fundamental logic of the system: the priority remained on structural liquidity management rather than attempting to predict market direction.

## 4. Management logic in May

The structure dynamics show that during May the system simultaneously:

- increased the USDT liquidity buffer (from 7,618 to 13,171);  
- kept the BTC amount constant at 0.137, accepting market price changes;  
- reduced the SOL amount from 51.23 to 44.515, slightly lowering its share in the overall structure;  
- reallocated part of the result into KEFEN, with KEFEN’s valuation in the pool rising from 77,650 to 90,752.

This behaviour is fully aligned with BitCapital principles: the priority is system robustness, liquidity redistribution, and preservation of the strategic KEFEN layer rather than maximising leverage or aggressively increasing risk.

## 5. Profit withdrawal and reserve formation

At the end of May, 12,000 USDT were withdrawn from the pool, while the remaining part of the result was proposed to be preserved in the form of a KEFEN reserve.

After these actions, the main pool at the beginning of June was brought back to the notional base of 100,000 USDT equivalent:

- USDT: 1,171  
- KEFEN: 197,406,000 at 0.0004307 (value 85,022)  
- BTC: 0.137 at 73,950 (value 10,131)  
- SOL: 44.515 at 82.6 (value 3,676)  

Separately, outside the main base, a BitCapital reserve was formed:

- KEFEN: 13,304,000 at 0.0004307 (value 5,730);  
- USDT: 2,460 (left after previous withdrawals and office expenses).

This way of structuring the result is consistent with the BitCapital priority hierarchy: first preserving and strengthening the system’s strategic resources (KEFEN and the liquidity reserve), and only then treating profit as available for withdrawal.

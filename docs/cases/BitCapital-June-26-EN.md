## 1. General Monthly Dynamics

June 2026 was a month of positive performance for BitCapital Research Pool under more mixed market conditions across the core assets. During the period, the pool value increased from the notional base of 100,000 to 112,613 USDT equivalent, which corresponds to a monthly result of 12.6% before profit distribution.

The gain was achieved without changing the basic system architecture: the initial result was generated through the revaluation of assets inside the pool, after which the profit was distributed between the system reserve and the two partners according to a predefined allocation ratio.

- 35% of the result was allocated to Partner 1;  
- 35% of the result was allocated to Partner 2.

After distribution, the main pool base was restored to 100,000 USDT equivalent for the start of the next period.

## 6. Management Logic in June

At the end of June, the system simultaneously:

- recorded a positive financial result at pool level;  
- distributed profit according to a predefined ratio between the reserve and the two partners;  
- preserved a separate strategic layer in the form of KEFEN;  
- restored the working base to a managed level for the next period.

This behavior is consistent with BitCapital principles: profit is not treated as fully available for immediate extraction, but is distributed within an internal priority architecture. The reserve strengthens the long-term resilience of the system, while partner payouts are made according to a predefined share of the actual result achieved.

The June report continues the logic of the January–May 2026 cases and confirms the consistency of the BitCapital approach:

From the perspective of the research pool, June 2026 can be viewed as a case not only of positive profitability, but also of disciplined result allocation. The month shows that BitCapital uses profit as a resource to strengthen the future resilience of the system while simultaneously applying a transparent distribution scheme between partners.

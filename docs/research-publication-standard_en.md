# Research Publication Standard

## Purpose of this Document

This document defines the publication standard for materials derived from the BitCapital Research Pool and related WLRT / WaveCounter-based applied research activity.

Its purpose is to ensure that research notes, case studies, technical observations, structural analyses, and educational materials are published in a consistent form that preserves methodological clarity while avoiding mischaracterization as financial advice, investment promotion, or client-facing performance communication.

## Scope

This standard applies to public and semi-public materials derived from:
- the BitCapital Research Pool;
- internal applied testing of WLRT and WaveCounter™ Liquidity Pool Management System (LPM);
- structural observations involving KEFEN and other Pool components;
- educational and methodological materials based on internal experiments.

This standard may be used for GitHub documents, website articles, research notes, reports, public summaries, archived materials, and explanatory publications.

## Core Principles

Materials published under this standard should follow the principles below:

1. **Method before outcome**  
   Publications should explain the structural logic, assumptions, and conditions of the observed system before emphasizing any outcomes.

2. **Observation before promotion**  
   Materials should present what was observed, tested, or modeled rather than encourage participation, acquisition, or imitation.

3. **Context before interpretation**  
   Reported results should be framed within the specific structure, role assignments, reserve logic, market regime, and operational assumptions in which they arose.

4. **Limits before claims**  
   Constraints, uncertainties, edge cases, and non-reproducibility risks should be stated clearly before drawing broader conclusions.

5. **Research before recommendation**  
   Published materials should retain a research and methodological tone and should not be framed as instructions to invest, trade, allocate, or acquire specific assets.

## Recommended Structure for Publications

A publication derived from the BitCapital Research Pool should, where appropriate, contain the following sections:

### 1. Title

The title should describe the subject of analysis rather than imply a financial result.

Preferred examples:
- Structural observation of reserve behavior in the BitCapital Research Pool
- Role reassignment under changing liquidity conditions
- Notes on KEFEN interaction inside a role-based liquidity system

Avoid titles that emphasize gains, winning trades, or investor appeal.

### 2. Context

The publication should explain:
- the internal and non-client nature of the BitCapital Research Pool;
- the methodological basis in WLRT / WaveCounter LPM;
- the structural conditions relevant to the observation;
- the fact that the material reflects internal research activity.

### 3. System Setup

The publication should describe the relevant setup as clearly as practical, including:
- the assets or components involved;
- role assignments, if relevant;
- reserve assumptions;
- the observed regime or structural state;
- whether the observation was manual, semi-automated, or otherwise executed.

The objective is not to reveal every internal detail, but to provide enough methodological context for the reader to understand what is being described.

### 4. Observation or Case

This section should describe what was observed.

It may include:
- structural changes;
- regime transitions;
- intervention logic;
- liquidity behavior;
- token-role interaction;
- reserve deployment or non-deployment.

The tone should remain descriptive and analytical.

### 5. Interpretation

Interpretation should remain bounded and conditional.

Preferred language includes formulations such as:
- under the tested conditions;
- within this specific structural configuration;
- in the observed regime;
- in this internal research setting.

Avoid absolute statements about universal effectiveness, predictable returns, or inevitable outcomes.

### 6. Limits and Risks

Each substantial publication should clearly state relevant limitations, including where applicable:
- market dependence;
- structural specificity;
- model or execution risk;
- incomplete reproducibility;
- uncertainty about transferability to external settings.

### 7. Non-Advisory Notice

Each publication should contain a short notice stating that the material is provided for research, educational, methodological, or technical purposes only and does not constitute investment advice, financial promotion, or an invitation to participate in any pooled activity.

## Language Guidance

### Preferred language

Use language such as:
- observed;
- tested;
- modeled;
- documented;
- structurally viable;
- role-based;
- reserve-dependent;
- internal research environment;
- methodological note;
- internal case material.

### Language to avoid

Avoid language such as:
- guaranteed;
- safe return;
- profitable setup;
- best trade;
- alpha signal;
- buy opportunity;
- investor access;
- passive income;
- low-risk yield.

Also avoid overly promotional formulations that could create investor expectations even if no explicit recommendation is made.

## Use of Performance Information

Performance-related information, if published at all, should be handled with caution.

If included, it should:
- be secondary to structural explanation;
- be accompanied by context, constraints, and interpretation limits;
- not be presented as marketing proof;
- not be framed as a basis for expectation of future results;
- not be isolated from the broader methodological conditions that produced it.

Where possible, structural behavior is preferable to headline performance numbers.

## Use of KEFEN in Publications

When KEFEN is referenced in published materials, the publication should aim to explain its functional role within the structure rather than frame it as a speculative opportunity.

Publications should make clear, where relevant, that KEFEN:
- is discussed as part of a structural and experimental liquidity environment;
- does not represent equity in BitCapital;
- does not create rights to pool assets or guaranteed returns;
- may have future utility or executive functions only if separately defined.

## Publication Channels

This standard may be applied across multiple publication channels, including:
- GitHub repositories;
- project websites;
- research archives;
- internal memoranda shared externally in limited form;
- long-form methodological notes;
- educational explainers.

The same methodological and non-promotional standard should be preserved across all such channels.

## Review Before Publication

Before publication, each substantial research note or case material should be reviewed against the following checklist:

- Does the text describe method and structure before outcomes?
- Does it clearly state the internal and research nature of the source material?
- Does it avoid promotional, investment-oriented, or client-facing language?
- Does it include limits, risks, and interpretive boundaries?
- Does it avoid creating expectations of future returns?
- Does it avoid presenting KEFEN or other assets as guaranteed opportunity or entitlement?
- Does it include a short non-advisory notice?

## Status of this Standard

This document is a working publication standard for the current stage of the BitCapital documentation ecosystem.

It may be expanded or revised as additional publication formats, operator-facing materials, software documentation layers, or governance-related materials are introduced.
